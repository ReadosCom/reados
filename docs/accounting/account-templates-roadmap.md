# Account Templates Roadmap (Post-V1)

V1 includes:
- Turkey Tek Düzen
- IFRS starter hierarchy
- US GAAP starter hierarchy

Potential standards and regional frameworks to add after V1:
- UK GAAP (FRS 102)
- French PCG
- German SKR03 / SKR04
- Spanish PGC
- Italian OIC chart mappings
- UAE IFRS-localized charts
- Saudi SOCPA mappings
- India Ind AS mappings
- Brazil CPC mappings
- Canada ASPE / IFRS variants
- Australia AASB mappings
- New Zealand NZ IFRS mappings
- South Africa IFRS for SMEs mappings
- Japan J-GAAP mappings
- China CAS mappings
- Mexico NIF mappings

Data-source approach:
- Prefer publicly licensed government/regulator/open-data chart templates when available.
- Where licensing is unclear or unavailable, maintain embedded templates and document provenance for legal review.
